What Affects the Rate?

Dynamic pricing helps keep access flexible while reflecting when simulator time is most in demand.

01

Time of Day

Prime evening hours generally price higher than early morning, midday, or late-night windows.

02

Day of Week

Weekend hours usually carry higher demand than standard weekday windows.

03

Season

Indoor golf demand tends to increase during colder months and offseason training periods.

04

Availability

Popular windows may price higher as demand increases and available bay time becomes more limited.

If you plan to play more than once in a while, prepaid packs or memberships may reduce your effective hourly cost.
